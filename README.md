I love making content for Minecraft and seeing something come to life from just an idea.

Whether it's having a complete SMP build-plan, or just a vague idea of something, I always love seeing what people have in-store.

### Pricing

As of now, I'm not charging for my services.

I make all my plugins, mods, and other things for free.

The reason for this is mostly because I enjoy development, experimentation, and helping others to create their own projects.
